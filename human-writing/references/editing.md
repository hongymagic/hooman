# Editing decisions

Read this for examples and document-specific judgement. Meaning takes precedence
over every pattern below. Examples in this guide are invented.

## Give the reader useful information

An opening earns its space when it tells the reader what happened, what to do or
why the subject matters to them. Remove an opening that could introduce any
document in the category.

Before:

> In today's fast-paced digital world, managing invoices is more important than ever.
> Our game-changing export helps teams unlock their potential.

After, given a brief that supports this behaviour:

> Export this month's invoices as a CSV from the Billing page.

The edit explains an actual action. Without that behaviour in the brief, ask or
narrow the claim; specificity cannot be invented. For a README, explain the
project, show a usable first command and state the requirements. For an incident
update, establish impact and current status. A personal essay can keep its reveal
when that sequence serves the piece.

## Edit empty devices in context

Before:

> This isn't just a cache update. It's a revolution in reliability.
> The cache stores responses for 60 seconds, not 60 requests.

After:

> The cache stores responses for 60 seconds, not 60 requests.

The second contrast explains a boundary and stays. A checker cannot make that
distinction reliably by shape alone. Useful reader questions stay for the same
reason: “What happens when the worker crashes after the write?” addresses a real
failure case. A question that merely introduces the next sentence does less work.

## Preserve commitments and uncertainty

Before:

> It is important to note that we may be able to ship on Friday, subject to
> Security approving the review. We have not approved the wider rollout.

After:

> We may be able to ship on Friday if Security approves the review.
> The wider rollout has not been approved.

The possibility, dependency and lack of approval survive. “We will ship Friday”
would change the commitment. “Security will approve it” would invent a forecast.
Keep business days, time zones, upper bounds and conditions just as carefully.

Distinguish observations, reports and measurements. Five customers reporting
easier searches does not establish faster searches. A staging result does not
establish production behaviour or causation. A numeric finding asks for
verification; it does not accuse the author of making up a number.

## Use direct verbs and connected sentences

Before:

> We will undertake an assessment of applications on Monday. We will provide
> notification of the decision to applicants by Friday.

After:

> We will assess applications on Monday and notify applicants of the decision
> by Friday.

Keep distinct actions distinct when they have different owners or dependencies.
Do not shorten a procedure by losing its order or an approval restriction.

Join choppy repetition when the ideas belong together; split a sentence when its
conditions become hard to follow. Punctuation and lists are available tools.
Three necessary steps, an em dash and passive voice with an unknown actor do not
need correction merely because they match a habit.

## Match a real voice

A genuine writing sample can guide formality, rhythm and humour. It cannot supply
facts for a new piece. Keep the author's dry aside or deliberate fragment when it
works. Do not add a joke, memory, opinion or error to make a document seem human.
For neutral technical prose, neutrality is appropriate. Do not turn every README
into a pitch or every email into clipped commands.

Keep courtesy that serves the relationship. In languages other than English,
follow the language's register and grammar instead of transplanting English
contractions or forbidden-word lists.
